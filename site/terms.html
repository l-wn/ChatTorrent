What ChatTorrent is

ChatTorrent is a free, non-commercial experiment run by lwn. It's an AI chatbot that runs in its users' browsers: each person who chooses to contribute runs part of the AI model on their own graphics card, and together they answer everyone's messages.

People use it in clusters. A cluster is a group of browser tabs that are open on ChatTorrent at the same time and run the AI model together, each on its own graphics card. The public demo is a cluster anyone can join. A private cluster is one that only people with its invite link can join.

You accept these terms when you press "Join and chat" or "Create cluster". Pressing either also confirms that you're 18 or over and that you'll follow the code of conduct below. If you don't agree, please don't use ChatTorrent.

An experiment, provided as it is

ChatTorrent is an experiment, and it's provided as it is, with no guarantees. It may be slow, stop working, lose your chats, or change without notice. It only works while enough people's graphics cards between them hold the whole model, and it may be shut down at any time.

AI-generated replies

Every reply comes from an AI model, not a person. Replies can be wrong, made up, out of date or offensive. They aren't professional advice of any kind, whether medical, legal, financial or anything else. Check anything important with a reliable source, and don't rely on ChatTorrent for decisions that matter.

Your GPU and connection

Contributing your graphics card is optional. You choose whether to, and how much memory to give, on the setup screen before you join, and you can change it later under Contribute. If you do, your browser downloads part of the AI model (several gigabytes) and uses your graphics card, electricity and internet connection to run other people's messages. You do this at your own risk, and any costs, such as your electricity or data usage, are yours. You can stop at any time by closing the tab.

Other people in your cluster

The AI runs in other people's browsers. They're independent: ChatTorrent doesn't control them or what they do, including whether they look at or record messages that pass through their browser, and isn't responsible for other users' actions. If you want to control who runs your chats, start a private cluster and share its invite link only with people you trust. See the privacy notice for what others can see.
